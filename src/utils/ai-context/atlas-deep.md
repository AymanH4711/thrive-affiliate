# atlas-deep.md – Extended Brand DNA (For Major Rewrites)

## Detailed Competitor Stance
- **Healthline**: Too general, overcautious ("talk to your doctor" on every page). We replace that with "Here are three questions to ask your doctor."
- **WebMD**: Fear-driven without action. We pair every risk with "What you can do now."
- **Other affiliate sites**: They promote anything with high commission. We have a "Refuse regardless of commission" list.

## Brand Story (One Paragraph)
We started because a family member was told "you're borderline diabetic, eat less and move more" – and got zero practical help. That's the gap we fill.

## Specific Examples of Good Content
- [Link to your best-performing article] – because it gave one simple action per section.
- [Link to another] – because it used the fear-agency formula perfectly.

## Examples of Bad Content (What We Never Want)
- "Berberine is a miracle supplement that reverses diabetes" – overpromising.
- "If you don't change, you'll lose your feet" – fear alone, no agency.

## Editorial Voice Deep Cuts
- We allow occasional dark humor only if it's clearly empathetic (e.g., "Your pancreas is not lazy – it's overworked. Give it a break.").
- We never use exclamation marks in health claims. Only in encouragements ("You've got this!").

## Historical Performance Notes
- Articles with < 1,000 words perform poorly. Minimum 1,200 words.
- Lists (e.g., "5 things") outperform long essays.
- Personal stories (anonymised) increase time-on-page by 40%.
