# echo.md – Brand Voice (Short Form)

## Core Tone (One Line)
Calm, precise, and quietly urgent – like a smart friend who actually read the studies.

---

## Do Say (Keep These Patterns)

**Opening hooks that work:**
> "Your A1C result isn't a verdict. It's a starting point."
> "That afternoon crash isn't your fault. But it is fixable."
> "Here's what your doctor meant to say (but didn't have time for)."

**Explaining mechanisms (simple > accurate > simple):**
> "Insulin is the key. Glucose is the lock. Prediabetes means the key is getting rusty."
> "Think of berberine like a gentle mechanic for that rusty key – not a replacement, just a helper."

**Giving actionable advice:**
> "Try this tomorrow morning: 10-minute walk after breakfast. That's it. No gym membership required."
> "Before you buy any supplement, check the label for 'berberine HCL' specifically. The other forms are weaker."

**Addressing fear without causing paralysis:**
> "Yes, high blood sugar damages blood vessels. But here's the part no one tells you: that damage slows – and sometimes stops – when glucose improves. You're not a lost cause."
> "Complications aren't a life sentence. They're a warning light. And warning lights mean you still have time."

**Disclaimers that don't feel like lawyers wrote them:**
> "Quick legal stuff: we're not your doctor. Nothing here replaces medical advice. But we will give you the questions to ask at your next appointment."

---

## Don't Say (Delete These Patterns Immediately)

| ❌ Don't | ✅ Instead |
|----------|------------|
| "It is important to maintain optimal glycemic control." | "Keep your blood sugar steady. Here's how." |
| "Consult your healthcare provider regarding medication adjustments." | "Ask your doctor: 'Would lowering my carbs help me reduce my metformin dose?'" |
| "A holistic approach to wellness optimization." | [Delete entirely – means nothing] |
| "Reverses insulin resistance permanently." | "Some studies suggest improvement within 12 weeks." |
| "Clinically proven to lower A1C by 2 points." | "One small study saw an average drop of 0.8% – we cite it below." |
| "The #1 supplement doctors recommend." | "One of the more studied options, according to PubMed." |

**Forbidden words (never use):**
synergistic, holistic (unless mocking it), optimize your wellness journey, superfoods, detox, cleanse (in supplement context), biohack.

---

## Sentence Rhythm
Short sentences for key claims. Longer for explanations. Never two long sentences in a row.

**Good:**
> "Metformin is cheap. It works. But it's not for everyone. Some people can't tolerate the digestive side effects – and that's not a failure. It's just biology."

**Bad:**
> "It is important to note that Metformin, while generally well-tolerated, may cause gastrointestinal distress in a subset of patients, which can be managed through dose titration." (Too long – reader glazes over.)

---

## Tone Under Pressure (Scary Topics)

**Formula:** Acknowledge → Pivot → Action (1 sentence each)

**Example:**
> "Unmanaged high blood sugar can damage nerves and kidneys. That's the honest truth. (Acknowledge)
> But here's the part most articles skip: those changes happen slowly. And they slow down further – sometimes stop – when you start managing glucose today. (Pivot)
> Pick one thing from this article and do it tomorrow morning. That's step one. (Action)"

Never end a scary paragraph with just fear.

---

## Quick Audit (Before Publishing)
1. Would a busy 55-year-old read this out loud without feeling stupid?
2. Does this sound like a brochure or a human? (If brochure, delete every "it is important to".)
3. Could an FTC lawyer find a problem with any claim? (If yes, add a qualifier.)
