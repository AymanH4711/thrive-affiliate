# atlas-core.md – Brand DNA (Short Form)

## Mission (1 sentence)
Give [newly diagnosed prediabetics / insulin-resistant adults] the [plain-English, actionable roadmap] their doctor didn't have time to provide.

## Ideal Customer Profile (Surgical)
- **Age/Gender**: [40-65, slight female skew ~60%]
- **Diagnosis stage**: [Prediabetic (A1C 5.7-6.4%) OR "borderline diabetic" – often undiagnosed and worried]
- **What they Googled last**: ["best breakfast for prediabetes", "can prediabetes be reversed", "metformin vs berberine"]
- **Primary fear**: [Medication dependency / "inevitable" type 2 diabetes / weight gain from insulin resistance]
- **What they tried (failed)**: [Blindly cutting carbs (unsustainable) / random supplements from Amazon / generic "eat less move more" advice]

## Niche Positioning (How we're different)
- **Vs Healthline/WebMD**: We don't write for a general audience. We write *only* for prediabetes. We don't say "consult your doctor" as a crutch – we give actionable next steps.
- **Vs other affiliate sites**: We don't promote products we wouldn't give a family member. No "miracle cures." Every supplement recommendation includes an **evidence badge** (Strong / Moderate / Weak).

## Content Rules (In/Out of Bounds)
**In scope**:
- Prediabetes reversal (diet, exercise, monitoring, sleep, stress)
- Supplement reviews with citations (berberine, inositol, chromium, etc.)
- Device comparisons (CGM vs fingerstick)
- Plain-English explanations of insulin resistance, A1C, glucose spikes

**Off limits (explicitly)**:
- We never recommend stopping or reducing prescribed medication without a doctor's supervision.
- We never claim any supplement "cures" diabetes or "reverses" insulin resistance definitively.
- No meal plans that are under 1200 calories.
- No fear-mongering about complications without pairing with agency ("Here's what you can do").

## Affiliate Boundaries
**Promote**:
- Verified third-party supplements (with lab testing visible)
- Glucose monitors (CGM, fingerstick)
- Evidence-based books / programs

**Refuse (regardless of commission)**:
- Unregulated proprietary blends without individual ingredient disclosure
- "Detox" or "cleanse" products targeting blood sugar
- MLM (multi-level marketing) supplement lines

## One Non-Negotiable Rule
Every article making a health claim must include at least **2 citations from PubMed / NIH / ADA** and display an **EvidenceStrengthBadge**.
